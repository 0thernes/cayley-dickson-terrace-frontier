# Explicit Epistemic Boundaries & Limitations (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This project separates machine-checked theorems (`[LEAN-6K]`), exact computational certificates (`[EXACT-COMP]`), and open mathematical frontiers (`[OPEN]`). The following problems and extensions are **not claimed solved or formally proved** by `v3.0.0`:

1. **General Sums-of-Squares Beyond Coordinate Subspaces (`ALG-035`, `[OPEN]`):**
   - Theorems `T3b`–`T3c` (Euclidean coordinate breakaway $n + 5 < \rho(2^n)$ for $n \ge 6$) and `T4e` ($r_{\max}(\mathrm{CD}_5) = 14$ with strict $7+7$ rigidity) govern *coordinate subsets* under native Euclidean Cayley–Dickson multiplication. They do **not** resolve the general real or field-dependent Hurwitz sums-of-squares problem $[r, s, N]$ (cf. Yiu 1990; Zhang–Zhu 2026), nor do they rule out non-coordinate linear subspaces of $\mathrm{CD}_n$ exceeding coordinate bounds.
2. **Even-Parity Higher-Blade ($k \ge 4$) Zero-Divisor Varieties (`ALG-039`, `[OPEN]`):**
   - Pillar 6 (`T6a`–`T6c`, `[LEAN-6K]`) completely excludes **all** integer elements $x \in \mathbb{Z}^{2^n}$ with **odd** coordinate sum — including **all** signed odd-support unit $k$-blades ($3$-blades, $5$-blades, $7$-blades, ...) — from being left or right zero divisors across every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and every signature $\gamma \in \{\pm 1\}^n$. However, **even-parity** higher blades ($k \ge 4$, such as the sharp 4-blade zero divisor $e_1 + e_2 + e_9 - e_{10}$ certified in Adversarial Test `A9`) DO produce zero divisors whose full annihilator stratification and algebraic varieties $Z(\mathrm{CD}_n)$ remain open.
3. **Exact Symmetric Coordinate Subspace Ceiling $r_{\max}(\mathrm{CD}_n)$ for $n \ge 6$ (`COMB-040`, `[OPEN]`):**
   - In `v3.0.0`, $r_{\max}(\mathrm{CD}_3) = 8$, $r_{\max}(\mathrm{CD}_4) = 10$ (`T4a`), $r_{\max}(\mathrm{CD}_5) = 14$ with strict $7+7$ rigidity (`T4e`), and the lower bound $r_{\max}(\mathrm{CD}_6) \ge 16$ (`T4d`) are all proved in Lean `[LEAN-6K]`. Determining exact $r_{\max}(\mathrm{CD}_n)$ for $n \ge 6$ and lifting the exact 280-subspace census `C2` into Lean remain open.
4. **All-$n$ Asymptotic Distinct Split-Phase Count (`[OPEN]`):**
   - Pillar 5 (`T5a`–`T5c`, `[LEAN-6K]`) completely resolves the all-$n$, all-signature interior spectral-gap classification for every $n \ge 6$ (proving the exact `iff` criterion $8 \mid f \land f \notin \{0, 8, 2^{n-1}, 2^{n-1}+8\}$ and exact count $2^{n-3} - 4$, plus the width $2^{n-3} - 1$ continuous band gap at $f = 2^n - 8$). Proving that the total number of distinct 2-blade nullity-histogram phases across all $2^n$ signatures equals $2^{n-2}$ for arbitrary $n \ge 6$ (certified in Lean for $n = 4, 5$ in `T4c` and exhaustively in Rust/C11/Python for $n = 6, 7, 8$ in `C1`) remains open.
5. **Classical External Theorems Referenced But Not Formalized in Lean:**
   - The Biss–Dugger–Isaksen (2008) general annihilator upper bound $\dim \mathrm{Ann}(x) \le 2^n - 4n + 4$ and the Hurwitz–Radon–Adams theorem establishing that $\rho(2^n) = 8\lfloor n/4\rfloor + 2^{n \bmod 4}$ equals the maximal orthogonal family dimension over $\mathbb{R}^{2^n}$ are classical published theorems cited as external context; Lean proves the exact algebraic bounds on our Cayley–Dickson objects and the exact arithmetic comparisons against those classical formulas (`T2b`, `T3c`).
