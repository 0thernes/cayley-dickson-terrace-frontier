# Claims & Verification Ledger (`CLAIMS.md`)

Every mathematical and computational claim in `cayley-dickson-terrace-frontier` (`0TH-2026-019`) is indexed below with its epistemic tag, machine-checked declaration or computational witness, and independent check (falsification test). The wording was corrected on 2026-11-26 to match an independent statement audit ([audit log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md); details in [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md)).

- **`[LEAN-6K]`**: Proved in zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35.0, audited via `VerifyAxioms.lean` within `{propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}`, kernel-checked by `lake build`, `leanchecker` and `leanchecker-paranoid`, and replayed in CI on 6 kernels (`lean`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`) via `lake check --paranoid` and `lake comparator --config comparator.json --paranoid`.
- **`[EXACT-COMP]`**: Verified with exact integer/rational arithmetic across independent implementations (Rust, ISO C11, Python), or by a zero-gap HiGHS MILP certificate, with frozen SHA-256 JSON artifacts in `data/`. Not proved in Lean.
- **`[OPEN]`**: Not claimed solved (see [`LIMITATIONS.md`](LIMITATIONS.md)).

**Scope of every `[LEAN-6K]` nullity claim.** Lean proves statements about the explicit Cayley–Dickson sign table (`cdSign`, `cdSignGamma`) and about block counts: $\nu_n^\gamma(i,j)$ is defined as half the number of singular $2\times 2$ blocks of left multiplication by $e_i+e_j$. That this count equals $\dim \mathrm{Ann}(e_i+e_j)$ is a lemma proved in the paper, not in Lean. Pillar 3 concerns the Euclidean signature and coordinate (basis-index) subsets only.

## Prior work to read alongside these claims

- Moreno: annihilator dimensions in Cayley–Dickson algebras are multiples of four (stated in Biss, Dugger, Isaksen, arXiv:math/0511691). `T2b`'s divisibility is the 2-blade case.
- de Marrais, *The 42 Assessors and the Box-Kites They Fly*, arXiv:math/0011260 (2000): the 42 sedenion assessors and 7 box-kites (`T4b`, the $n = 4$ case of `T2f`).
- Zhilina, *Orthogonality graphs of real Cayley–Dickson algebras. Part I*, Int. J. Algebra Comput. 31 (2021); arXiv:2608.28176: classification of zero divisors whose components are standard basis elements up to sign. Overlap with `T1c`, `T2a`, `T2c` not assessed.
- Moreno, arXiv:math/0404395: characterization of alternative elements of the Cayley–Dickson algebras; related to, but not the same as, the coordinate condition in `T3a`.
