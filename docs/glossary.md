# Mathematical & Verification Glossary (`docs/glossary.md`)

- **2-Blade (`e_i + e_j`):** A sum of two distinct imaginary canonical basis elements ($1 \le i < j < 2^n$) in $\mathrm{CD}_n(\gamma)$.
- **2-Blade Nullity (`twobladeNullityGamma`, $\nu_n^\gamma(i,j)$):** The exact dimension $\dim \ker L_{e_i+e_j}$ of the left-annihilator of $e_i + e_j$ in $\mathrm{CD}_n(\gamma)$, proved in Lean (`T1f`) via an explicit $\mathbb{Z}$-basis isomorphism to equal half the number of singular $2\times 2$ blocks $\{e_k, e_{k\oplus i\oplus j}\}$.
- **Bad Affine 2-Plane:** A 4-element affine 2-plane $\{a, b, c, d\} \subset \mathbb{F}_2^n$ ($a \oplus b \oplus c \oplus d = 0$) on which at least one of its three unordered pairings fails bilinear norm cancellation $\sigma(a,c)\sigma(b,d) + \sigma(a,d)\sigma(b,c) = 0$. In $\mathrm{CD}_4$, every bad affine 2-plane splits strictly $2+2$ across $\{1..7\}$ and $\{9..15\}$ (`T4a`).
- **BDI Ceiling (`bdiMaxAnnihilator`):** The Biss--Dugger--Isaksen (2008) upper bound $2^n - 4n + 4$ on $\dim \mathrm{Ann}(x)$ for nonzero $x \in \mathrm{CD}_n$ ($n \ge 4$). Coincides with the 2-blade ceiling uniquely at $n = 4$ and separates strictly for all $n \ge 5$ (`T2b`).
- **Box-Kite (`7 × K_{2,2,2}`):** One of the 7 disjoint octahedral connected components (6 vertices, 12 edges each) of the 42 sedenion zero-divisor assessors discovered by de Marrais (2000), partitioned by XOR strut signature $s = i \oplus j \in \{9,\dots,15\}$ (`T4b`).
- **Flip Mask (`flip(γ)`):** An $n$-bit integer encoding signature $\gamma \in \{\pm 1\}^n$ where bit $r$ is `1` iff doubling step $r$ is split ($\gamma_r = +1$) and `0` iff Euclidean ($\gamma_r = -1$).
- **Hurwitz--Radon Function (`ρ(2^n)`):** For $n = 4a + b$ ($0 \le b \le 3$), $\rho(2^n) = 8a + 2^b$, governing the maximal real composition formula $[2^n, \rho(2^n), 2^n]$ (Hurwitz 1922, Radon 1922).
- **Interior Spectral Gap & All-$n$ Dichotomy (`T4f`, `T5a`–`T5c`):** A nullity multiple of $4$ strictly between $0$ and $2^{n-1}$ with zero count $H_n^\gamma(v) = 0$ in a split-signature algebra $\mathrm{CD}_n(\gamma)$. First emerges at dimension $64$ (`H_6^{56}(20) = 0`, `T4f`); for every $n \ge 4$, `T5c` proves an interior mod-4 gap exists iff $8 \mid f \land f \notin \{0, 8, 2^{n-1}, 2^{n-1}+8\}$ (exactly $2^{n-3}-4$ gapped masks), with $\mathrm{flip} = 2^n - 8$ opening an exponential continuous band gap of $2^{n-3}-1$ empty bins (`T5b`).
- **Mirror-Pair Conservation:** Universal invariance of $H_n^\gamma(v) + H_n^\gamma(2^{n-1}-v)$ across all $2^n$ signatures $\gamma$ (Theorem `T1e`).
- **Odd-Parity Zero-Divisor Exclusion (`T6a`–`T6c`):** Universal theorem showing that in any twisted group algebra $\mathbb{Z}[\mathbb{Z}_2^n]^\sigma$ with arbitrary $\{\pm 1\}$ cocycle $\sigma$ (including all $\mathrm{CD}_n(\gamma)$), any $x \in \mathbb{Z}^{2^n}$ with odd coordinate sum has an exact odd-determinant integral left/right inverse (`B_L L_x = Δ_L I`, `B_R R_x = Δ_R I`, $\Delta$ odd), excluding all odd-sum zero divisors and all odd-support signed unit $k$-blades ($k = 3, 5, 7, \dots$).
- **Palomar Comparator:** Statement-fidelity verification protocol comparing `PalomarChallenge` (29 targets) against sorry-free `PalomarSolution` across 6 independent Lean 4.35.0 proof kernels (`lean`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`).
- **Pathion $7+7$ Rigidity (`T4e`):** Pure-kernel Lean theorem proving over all $2^{32}$ subsets of $\mathrm{CD}_5$ that every symmetric norm-multiplicative coordinate subspace satisfies $|S| \le 14$, and every maximal 14D subspace splits strictly $7+7$ across the lower and upper sedenion halves (with 280 such subspaces total, all containing $\{e_0, e_{16}\}$).
- **Right-Compatibility Graph (`G_n^comp`):** Graph on $\{0, \dots, 2^n - 1\}$ with an edge between distinct $j, x$ iff $(j, x)$ is universally compatible on the right against all of Euclidean $\mathrm{CD}_n$; its exact clique number is $\omega(G_n^{\mathrm{comp}}) = n + 5$ for all $n \ge 3$ (`T3b`).
